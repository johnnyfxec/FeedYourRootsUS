# Hooks verbales — ejemplos ya traducidos al avatar Morgan (FYR)

`knowledge/hooks_verbales_20_v2.md` es la fuente del principio psicológico, la regla de uso y la estructura de cada uno de los 20 hooks — eso es universal y no se traduce. Pero sus "5 ejemplos" por hook están escritos 100% para el avatar de trading/libertad financiera (Converso Academy) porque ese documento es compartido con el otro negocio de Johnny y no se reescribe aquí.

Este archivo existe para que `fyr-content` no tenga que traducir cada ejemplo de trading a Morgan de memoria en cada sesión, arriesgando que se filtre vocabulario del dominio equivocado ("ingreso digital", "sueldo", "trading", "DMs"). Da 2 ejemplos ya traducidos por hook — en inglés, porque el copy final de FYR siempre es en inglés (mercado US) — para usar como punto de partida o referencia de tono, no como copy final a copiar literal.

**Avatar Morgan:** 28-42, patio suburbano o lote pequeño, busca soberanía alimentaria. El dolor no es "depender de un sueldo" — es "depender del supermercado / no saber qué come su familia / sentir que la autosuficiencia es para gente con 5 acres".

### 1. Negativo
- "The way you're using that patio right now guarantees it looks the same next spring."
- "This one habit is the real reason your garden never makes it past June."

### 2. Pregunta
- "When's the last time you checked where your food actually came from before you ate it?"
- "If the grocery store ran out tomorrow, how many days could your yard actually feed you?"

### 3. Numérico
- "90% of people who start a garden quit by week six. Here's the one reason."
- "You don't need 5 acres. You need 480 square feet. Here's the math."

### 4. Storytelling
- "A year ago I had a dead patch of grass and zero idea what I was doing. Today I'll show you what changed — and it wasn't more land."
- "My neighbor has ten times the yard I do. This is what happened when I stopped comparing."

### 5. Error común
- "The most common mistake people make with their first raised bed is picking the spot that looks nicest, not the one that gets sun."
- "Thinking you need to go big before you start is the mistake that keeps most backyards empty."

### 6. Secreto
- "What nobody tells you about growing your own food when you have a 9-to-5 and zero free time."
- "The thing every self-sufficient family does that has nothing to do with how much land they have."

### 7. Contraste/Versus

- "Buying seeds vs saving seeds: the math nobody shows you."

### 8. Autoridad
- "After helping families turn suburban yards into real food sources, I found they all made the same mistake at the start."
- "I've watched dozens of backyards go from empty grass to feeding a family. The pattern isn't what you'd think."

### 9. Predicción

### 10. Dolor/Frustración

### 12. Curiosidad
