Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 21, 2026

1. Who We Are

Feed Your Roots ("we," "us," "our") provides digital educational resources about small-space food production, operated under the laws of the State of Delaware. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

2. Information We Collect

Purchase information, collected and processed by Hotmart, our payment and delivery platform, when you buy a bundle. This includes your name, email address, and payment details, which Hotmart handles under its own privacy policy.

Email address, if you sign up for updates, contact us directly, or receive automated delivery of a purchased resource via hello@feedyourroots.us.

Basic site analytics (pages viewed, general location by region, device type), if analytics tools are active on feedyourroots.us, used only in aggregate to understand how the site is used.

We do not collect payment card details directly — all payment processing happens through Hotmart's systems, not ours.

3. How We Use Information

We use the information above to: deliver purchased resources, respond to support requests, send occasional updates about new resources (only if you've opted in), and understand site usage in aggregate to improve content.

4. What We Don't Do

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your email address with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

5. Third-Party Services

Purchases are processed through Hotmart, a third-party e-commerce platform. Your payment and delivery data is subject to Hotmart's own privacy policy, available at hotmart.com. We are not responsible for how Hotmart or other third-party services handle data outside our direct control.

6. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting hello@feedyourroots.us. Residents of certain states (including California) may have additional rights under state privacy law; we will honor applicable requests to the extent required.

7. Data Retention

We retain contact information only as long as necessary to fulfill purchases, respond to inquiries, or comply with legal obligations.

8. Children's Privacy

Our resources are intended for adult purchasers. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date above.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy: hello@feedyourroots.us
