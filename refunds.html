Refund Policy

Effective Date: August 21, 2026

Please Read Before You Purchase

Every Feed Your Roots resource is a digital file, delivered instantly to your inbox the moment your payment is confirmed. Because access is immediate and the content can be downloaded, printed, and kept permanently within seconds of purchase, we ask that you review the bundle contents carefully — what's included, what it covers, and whether it fits what you're looking for — before completing your purchase. Our Tools Preview shows the real cover of every resource in every bundle, precisely so you can see exactly what you're getting before you buy.

How Refunds Work

Purchases are processed through Hotmart, our payment and delivery platform. Hotmart sets a guarantee period for each product, currently 7 days from the date of purchase, during which a refund can be requested directly through Hotmart under their standard purchase terms — shown to you at checkout. We do not process refunds directly on feedyourroots.us; all requests go through Hotmart's refund system.

Why We Ask You to Consider Carefully

Unlike a physical product, a digital guide can't be "returned" once it's been downloaded — the value has already been delivered. We built Feed Your Roots to be genuinely useful from the first read, and we'd rather you buy the right bundle the first time than buy on impulse and request a refund later. If you're unsure which bundle is right for you, reach out before purchasing — we're glad to help you choose.

Requesting a Refund

If you're still within the guarantee period and wish to request a refund, this is handled entirely through Hotmart's refund portal, not through Feed Your Roots directly. You can find instructions in the purchase confirmation email Hotmart sent you, or by visiting Hotmart's support site.

Questions Before You Buy

If you're not sure a bundle is right for your situation — your space, your zone, your goals — email us at hello@feedyourroots.us before purchasing. We'd genuinely rather answer a question upfront than process a refund afterward.
